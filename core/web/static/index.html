      Case study edition

      Welcome to Human Evaluation

      Complete six ten-minute sessions. Each session prepares 20 blinded comparisons across three difficulty levels; answer as many as you can. External validation outcomes remain hidden.

      1. Open the study From the NeuroDiscovery menu, choose Human Evaluation 1 and enter the study password supplied by the organizer.
      2. Identify yourself Check the suggested name or ID, choose whether generator scores are shown, and start the next available session.
      3. Answer questions Compare the highlighted difference, plain-language terms, and five literature cards, then choose left, right, or Cannot judge.
      4. Save progress Answers save automatically. Open the question directory to jump, revisit, or change an answer at any time.
      5. Finish and export After ten active minutes, export the session JSON and send it to the organizer; repeat until all six sessions are complete.

      Controls available during every session

      Read evidence efficientlyUse the 1–5 tabs or View all. The 1–10 value rates evidence support, not replication probability; expand source text or study details, or open the paper when needed.
      Navigate and revisePress D for the question directory. It shows answered questions and difficulty; jump back to revise, and drag its edge to resize it.
      Continue safelyClose returns to NeuroDiscovery and preserves unfinished work; closing the app or computer does too. Next time, continue or delete the record and restart. The password is requested once per app launch.
      Language and shortcutsSwitch English or Chinese at any time. Use 1 / 2 / 3 to answer, D for the directory, and Left / Right Arrow while a literature number is focused.
      Ⅱ
      Pause before stepping away If you need to leave midway, click Pause first. Paused time is excluded from active answering time.
