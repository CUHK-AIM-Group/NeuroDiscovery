Before you begin

Your task

Use the plain-language hypothesis facts and standardized evidence cards to predict which candidate is more likely to replicate. You do not need specialist clinical knowledge.

What is recordedThe result file includes your answers, active decision time, answer changes, question difficulty, and study metadata.

How to complete the study

  1. Check the session setupConfirm your name or ID and whether generator scores are shown. You can switch English or Chinese at any time.
  2. Review both evidence cardsStart with the highlighted difference. Use the 1–5 literature tabs or View all. The 1–10 value rates evidence support, not replication probability; expand details or source text when needed.
  3. Choose one answerSelect the side more likely to replicate. If you cannot choose, record whether evidence is insufficient or the question is outside your expertise.
  4. Use the question directoryOpen it with D to jump between questions, check answer status and difficulty, or revise an answer. Drag its edge to resize it.
  5. Pause or continue laterOnly active study time counts. Pause before stepping away. Closing the study, app, or computer preserves unfinished work; reopen to continue or delete it and restart.
  6. Complete and export six sessionsEach session ends automatically after ten active minutes. Export its JSON result file and send it to the organizer, then start the next session.
